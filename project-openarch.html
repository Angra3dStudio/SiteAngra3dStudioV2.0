Open Arch

Creativity meets comfort


Open Arch is a private office project that blends design, functionality, and well-being into a space built for discerning professionals. It’s ideal for anyone seeking an inspiring, efficient environment that offers both privacy and sophistication when meeting with clients.


At the heart of the office, a spacious desk provides flexibility for various tasks and meetings. Natural elements, like strategically placed greenery, bring a sense of freshness and help create a healthier, more stimulating atmosphere.


Floor-to-ceiling curtains allow full control over natural light, adapting the space to your needs throughout the day. Last but not least, an immersive sound system sets the tone – perfect for deep focus or relaxing between tasks.

Elegance and calm


Large bookcases do more than organize, they subtly divide the office from the meeting room, creating a sense of flow while maintaining visual harmony. The reception area was designed for comfort and conversation, featuring a generous sofa and a refined armchair, ideal for breaks, reading, or strategic meetings.


One of Open Arch’s standout features is the set of elegant double doors that open onto the balcony, allowing natural light and an exceptional view to become part of your daily routine.


More than just an office, this space was created to boost productivity and make every moment at work more enjoyable and inspiring.
