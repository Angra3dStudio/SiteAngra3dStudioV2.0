Loft 53

The warmth of home

Discover Loft 53, a space designed for young couples seeking elegance and functionality. With spacious rooms, contemporary design, and large windows that fill every corner with natural light, this apartment combines comfort and modernity in a welcoming ambiance.

The aroma of coffee

Integrated with the living room, the kitchen features a central island connected to the dining area, creating an open layout that enhances the space and encourages interaction. Elegant cabinetry and the view through the expansive windows set the perfect scene for meals and memorable moments with friends or business meetings.

Peaceful sleep

The bedroom is a serene retreat, with minimalist decor and details that expand the space. Walls give way to a beautiful guardrail, creating lightness and openness, with a superior view of the entire home.

A charming reading nook invites relaxation, while the glass-door closet keeps the space organized and sophisticated.

To complete the look, the entrance to the bathroom, located just before the stairs, adds a touch of elegance to the space. And when you lie down, enjoy the privileged view provided by the floor-to-ceiling windows – a perfect invitation to decompress.
